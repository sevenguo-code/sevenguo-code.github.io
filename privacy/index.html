Privacy

This website uses first-party analytics only after a visitor accepts analytics. The purpose is to understand which research topics and links are useful to visitors.

Information collected

For page views and clicks, the site may record the page, clicked element, link destination, click coordinates, screen and viewport size, browser and device information, language, timezone, referrer, timestamp, and an anonymous browser session identifier. The hosting platform may supply approximate country, region, city, latitude, and longitude.

Information not collected

The site does not request precise GPS location and does not intentionally store names, email addresses, form content, or raw IP addresses in the analytics database.

Your choice

Visitors may decline analytics. The preference is stored in the browser and can be changed from Analytics settings in the website footer. Browser Do Not Track settings are respected.
