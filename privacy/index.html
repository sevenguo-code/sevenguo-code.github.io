Privacy

This website uses Google Analytics 4 to understand which research topics and links are useful to visitors.

Information collected

For page views and interactions, Google Analytics may record the page, clicked element, link destination, click coordinates, browser and device information, language, referrer, timestamp, and approximate country, region, and city derived from the visitor's network address.

Information not collected

The site does not request precise GPS location and does not intentionally collect names, email addresses, form content, exact addresses, or raw IP addresses. Google states that IP addresses used to derive approximate location are discarded before analytics data is logged.

Browser preference

Browser Do Not Track settings are respected. Visitors may also block or clear analytics cookies through their browser settings.
