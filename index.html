Research program

A high-throughput in situ structural biology platform for bacterial signaling

By integrating high-throughput, high-resolution imaging with structure-guided mutagenesis and functional analyses, my lab will connect the native molecular states and spatial organization of chemosensory arrays to bacterial motility and environmental adaptation. Beyond chemotaxis, this platform will enable systematic analysis of diverse molecular machines, linking native molecular architecture to cellular behavior and physiological function. Together, these studies will establish general principles by which bacteria sense and respond to environmental signals while providing a biological and technological framework for applications ranging from antimicrobial development to biomanufacturing.
